## Annex A — Full-size diagrams

The two pages that follow are the pin assignment and system wiring diagrams reproduced at full page size for printing. Print them landscape and keep them at the bench while wiring — the inline versions in §4.1 and §4.5 are reference copies only.

Editable sources: `schemes/pinout_esp32.svg` and `schemes/wiring_system.svg`. Both are generated by `schemes/make_pinout.py` and `schemes/make_wiring.py`; edit the pin list at the top of the script and re-run rather than redrawing by hand.
